khajabad

An offline PDF library for Malayalam devotional books. Cream paper, mint surfaces, deep‑ink type — and motion that settles like water.

  • Kotlin + Jetpack ComposeFoundation, UI and Animation only — no Material components or theme.
  • Motion languageStop‑motion letter hops, spring settles, 3D page turns, ripple counters.
  • ReaderPdfRenderer rasterisation, pinch/double‑tap zoom, thumbnails, bookmarks, progress.
  • Real contentBoth uploaded PDFs are bundled in app/src/main/assets/books.

Tap around the phone — every screen is live. Swipe the reader page, tap the counter, open the drawer.
